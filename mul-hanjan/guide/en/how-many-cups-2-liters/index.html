How many cups is 2 liters of water?

About 8½ cups, or four 16.9 oz bottles.

Published 2026-11-21

Quick answer: 2 liters of water is about 67.6 fl oz. That's about 8½ cups (8 oz each), 4 standard bottles (16.9 oz / 500 ml), or about 1.7 of a 40 oz tumbler.

"Drink 2 liters a day" is easy to say, but most of us in the US think in ounces, cups and bottles. Here's how it adds up with the containers you actually use.

What size is a "cup" or a "glass"?

  • 1 cup (US measuring cup) = 8 fl oz ≈ 237 ml
  • Regular drinking glass = often around 12 oz ≈ 355 ml (glasses vary a lot)
  • Standard water bottle = 16.9 fl oz = 500 ml
  • Tumblers = 20 oz ≈ 592 ml · 32 oz ≈ 946 ml · 40 oz ≈ 1,183 ml

1 US fluid ounce is about 29.6 ml, so 1 liter is about 33.8 fl oz.

2 liters vs. the "8×8 rule"

You may have heard "eight 8-ounce glasses a day." That's 64 fl oz, or about 1.89 liters, a little under 2 liters. To reach a full 2 liters you'd add about half a cup more.

The 8×8 rule is a handy rule of thumb, not an official requirement. How much you need depends on your body, the weather, how active you are, and how much water you get from food.

FAQ

How many 8 oz cups are in 2 liters?

About 8½. One US cup is 8 fl oz (about 237 ml), and 2,000 ÷ 236.6 ≈ 8.45 cups.

How many ounces is 2 liters?

About 67.6 US fluid ounces. One liter is about 33.8 fl oz.

How many water bottles make 2 liters?

Four standard 16.9 oz (500 ml) bottles make 2 liters.

Is 64 oz the same as 2 liters?

Close, but not quite. 64 fl oz is about 1.89 liters, a little under 2 liters.

How many 40 oz tumblers is 2 liters?

About 1.7 tumblers. A 40 oz tumbler holds about 1.18 liters when full.

Do I have to drink all 2 liters as plain water?

Not necessarily. Other drinks and the water in food count toward your total too. See Does tea count as water intake? If you have a health condition or need to limit fluids, talk to a doctor.
