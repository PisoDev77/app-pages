One Glass Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 (English version of the November 6, 2026 policy)

One Glass ("the app", 물 한 잔 in Korean) has no sign-up and never sends your water logs off your device.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number. There is no account or login. To show the ads described in section 3, Google AdMob uses your device's advertising ID.

2. Information stored in the app

Your water logs (amount, time, cup and drink type) and settings (daily goal, cup sizes, reminder times) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them from your device.

3. Third parties, ads and analytics

Your water logs and settings are never shared with anyone.

The app shows Google AdMob ads: banner ads on some screens, and short ads you can choose to watch to unlock a cup. To do this, Google collects the advertising ID and device and app information (such as device type, operating system, app version, and approximate location estimated from IP address) to serve ads and measure their performance.

  • Turn off personalized ads: phone Settings → Google → Ads → Delete (or reset) advertising ID
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The app uses no other analytics tools.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration when you log a drink.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: only when you turn on water reminders in the app's Settings, to show reminders scheduled on your device. Reminder text and times are decided on your device and are not sent anywhere. You can turn them off anytime in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Internet and advertising ID: to load and show ads (your water logs are never sent).

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the new effective date on this page.
